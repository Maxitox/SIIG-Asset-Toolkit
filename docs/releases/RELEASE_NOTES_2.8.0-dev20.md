## Evidence discipline

Raw mnemonic ratios are treated as fingerprints, not proof of semantic change. The tiny June→retail raw differences in GIGANTIC UFO and UNKNOWN reduce to relocated global-address materialization and are recorded as semantically unchanged. The meaning of LADYBUG's newly guarded global state `0x10` remains deliberately unresolved. No executable bytes, decompiled proprietary code, IPA/PKG/XEX content, or gameplay footage are bundled.
