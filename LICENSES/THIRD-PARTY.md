# Third-Party and External Material

Third-party material is **not automatically relicensed under the project WTFPL**.

For each external component, record its source, original license, version or date, and how it is used.

| Component | Source | Original license | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xenia-related material | Xenia Project | BSD-3-Clause | See bundled notice where applicable |
| Other dependencies | TBD | TBD | Record before redistribution |

Where a component's license permits redistribution but requires notices, preserve those notices.

Where redistribution rights are unclear, do not treat the material as project-owned merely because it is present in a working tree.
